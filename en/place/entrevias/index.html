Entrevías

Neighbourhood · Entrevías, Puente de Vallecas

Entrevías is a neighbourhood of Madrid belonging to the district of Puente de Vallecas. As of 2019, it was the poorest neighborhood in the municipality. According to the National Institute of Statistics, the population of Entrevías in 2006 was 37,790.
