Naval Museum of Madrid

Museum · Retiro · Museo Naval

The Naval Museum (Spanish: Museo Naval) is a naval museum in Madrid, Spain, devoted to the history of the Spanish Navy since the Catholic Monarchs, in the 15th century, up to the present. It is one of the National Museums of Spain and it is attached to the Ministry of Defence. The displays set naval history in a wide context with information about the history of the Spanish Empire.
